Design intent
Show chains only when they are useful.

Cards stay compact. A small stack badge is the only always-visible affordance. Hovering or focusing any card in a chain opens a local “chain lens”: related cards are highlighted, unrelated cards fade, and only the active chain gets temporary guide lines.

No spaghetti modeLines are hidden by default. They appear only for the hovered/pinned chain and disappear when focus leaves.
Goals, not ticketsThe overlay shows goal progression and workspace stack ancestry. Linear issue keys are supporting metadata, not the primary structure.
Linear only for MVPEvery chain is one ordered sequence. The next workspace is created from the previous goal; no forks, parallel lanes, or custom dependency editing.
